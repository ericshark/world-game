# World Game — Data

All geography is generated by `npm run build:data` (`scripts/build-countries.mjs`) and committed
in `public/data/`. Rebuild it after editing any file in `data/`.

## Sources

- **Shapes:** Natural Earth 1:10m admin-0 countries (public domain), dissolved and simplified
  with mapshaper. Micro-states keep full detail, because simplifying collapses atolls (Kiribati
  would lose Tarawa).
- **Population and GDP:** Natural Earth `POP_EST` and `GDP_MD` (about 2019). They are
  approximate, so game rules never depend on fine distinctions; for example, Rare Earth accepts
  answers near a threshold either way.
- **Areas:** measured from the unsimplified shapes. The tiny micro-states use official areas
  from `areaKm2Overrides`.
- **Capitals:** Natural Earth populated places, curated into `data/capitals.json`.

## Generated files

- `public/data/countries.json` holds the shapes plus `id`, `name`, `playable`, `status`,
  `region` and `isMicro` for hit-testing and highlighting.
- `public/data/sub/<ID>.json` holds one lazy admin-1 pack per country (`USA`, `CAN`, `MEX`, `BRA`,
  `AUS`, `IND`, `CHN`, `DEU`), generated by `npm run build:sub` (`scripts/build-subdivisions.mjs`)
  from Natural Earth 1:10m states and provinces: simplified shapes, label points, area, neighbours,
  aliases, shared internal border lines and an area-scaled zero-points distance.
- `public/data/facts.json` holds facts for the 197 playable places. Each entry includes:
  - subregion, population, area, label point and hemispheres;
  - neighbors, capitals, coasts and aliases.

  The file also contains:
  - a border-to-border distance matrix;
  - World Journey crossing exclusions;
  - ambiguous names.

## Curated files

| File                  | Contents                                                                                     |
| --------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `data/places.json`    | Playable places with display name, status and region                                         |
| `data/capitals.json`  | Capitals; the first one is the main capital, and every one counts as a hit                   |
| `data/aliases.json`   | Extra names players type (UK, DRC, Burma…), plus names that fit several countries            |
| `data/geography.json` | Landlocked, island nations, coasts, neighbor fixes, label and area fixes, familiarity nudges |

## Policies

- **Neutral naming.** Disputed areas that are not playable (Western Sahara, the Siachen
  Glacier) stay separate, and clicking them reports their own name. Because of this, Morocco and
  Mauritania do not share a border in the game.
- **Overseas land borders count.** France borders Brazil and Suriname through French Guiana,
  which makes it a legitimate rare answer. World Journey does not let you cross those borders
  (`journeyExclusions`), because that would teleport you across the Atlantic.
- **Neighbors** come from borders the shapes share. `neighborOverrides` restores the borders that
  simplification hides: the Vatican City and Italy enclave, and Ceuta and Melilla between Spain
  and Morocco.
- **Landlocked** follows the UN list plus Kosovo (45 countries). Countries on the Caspian Sea
  count as landlocked, but the Caspian is still listed as one of their coasts.
- **Island nations** are sovereign states whose main territory is islands, including islands
  they share with another state (Haiti, Ireland, Timor-Leste). Australia is a continent, so it is
  not an island nation.
- **Coasts** are `strict`, meaning commonly agreed. `lenient` coasts are reached only through a
  marginal sea, such as Belgium on the Atlantic via the North Sea. Lenient answers are accepted but
  never required.
- **Capitals.** Countries with several official capitals accept all of them (Bolivia, South
  Africa, the Netherlands, Malaysia, and others). For Israel and Palestine, the game uses the seat
  of government it lists, and nearby alternatives also count.
- **Familiarity** blends Natural Earth's label rank with population, GDP and area. A few
  hand-tuned nudges are in `familiarityAdjust`. It ranks how well known a country is, for
  difficulty tiers and Rare Earth rarity. It is an estimate, not player data.
